# Douyin Platform-Safe Wording

Use this reference when creating or reviewing Douyin finance/value-investing content. Treat this as a safety checklist, not a guaranteed official banned-word list. Platform rules and enforcement change over time; when uncertain, prefer conservative, educational, non-promotional wording.

## Core Principle

Write like an educational creator, not a salesperson, investment adviser, medical adviser, or traffic broker.

Prefer:

- "我的学习笔记"
- "商业模式观察"
- "不荐股，不预测，不构成投资建议"
- "一个理解框架"
- "值得继续研究"

Avoid:

- "稳赚"
- "保本"
- "必赚"
- "闭眼入"
- "百分百"
- "加我"
- "私聊"
- "进群"

## High-Risk Categories

### Absolute / Extreme Claims

Avoid:

- 最、第一、唯一、顶级、极致、极品
- 全网最低、史低、NO.1
- 永久、零风险、百分百、彻底、永不
- 之王、绝无仅有、独一无二
- 人手必备、全民都买、闭眼入、不踩雷

Prefer:

- "比较典型"
- "很有代表性"
- "值得研究"
- "我个人理解"
- "在某些场景下"
- "可能更适合"

### Off-Platform Contact / Traffic Diversion

Avoid:

- 微信、V、VX、QQ、手机号、电话、二维码
- 私聊、私我、进群、粉丝私群
- 主页找我、线下交易、私下转账
- 淘宝、拼多多、公众号、小程序
- 货到付款、同城自提

Prefer:

- "评论区交流"
- "公开留言"
- "后续我会继续做一期"
- "下一期展开"

Special handling:

- Do not say "加微信", "加V", "私我进群".
- If discussing WeChat as a company/product case, prefer "社交软件", "国民级社交工具", or "绿泡泡" when a brand-neutral/social-media style expression is enough.
- Use "微信" only when the exact company/product case is necessary, and never combine it with contact guidance.

### Finance / Investing

Avoid:

- 稳赚、保本、零风险、必赚、兜底
- 百分百盈利、内部渠道、内幕消息
- 只涨不跌、快速暴富、投资必回本
- 保本理财、目标价、应该买、必须买
- 抄作业、跟买、跟投、带单

Prefer:

- "不荐股，不预测，不构成投资建议"
- "学习框架"
- "商业模式研究"
- "估值需要另行判断"
- "风险和收益都要自己承担"
- "这里只讨论生意，不讨论买卖点"

Plain-language replacements:

- ROE → "公司用一块钱本金能赚多少钱"
- 自由现金流 → "赚到的钱是不是真能留下来"
- 护城河 → "别人想抢它生意难不难"
- 定价权 → "成本涨了，它能不能涨价，消费者还愿意买"

### Alcohol / Tobacco

Avoid promotional tone:

- 喝酒建议、劝酒、豪饮、必喝、酒量、上头
- 未成年人相关饮酒画面或暗示
- 香烟/烟草的美化表达、成瘾浪漫化

Prefer:

- "酒类"
- "含酒精饮品"
- "8+1" only as mild platform slang for category-level discussion, not as a way to promote drinking or bypass rules.
- "烟草案例"
- "依赖型产品"
- "涉及健康、监管和伦理风险"

Rules:

- When using Moutai/Diageo/tobacco as business cases, keep the angle on business model, pricing power, regulation, and risk.
- Always add a boundary when mentioning tobacco: "好生意不等于好产品，也不等于没有健康、监管和伦理风险。"
- Do not depict minors, excessive drinking, smoking glamor, or purchase guidance.

### Medical / Health Claims

Avoid:

- 治愈、根治、消炎、降三高、降糖、降压
- 防癌、排毒、祛斑祛痘、医用级、药妆
- 无副作用、100%见效、消肿、止痛
- 增强免疫力、祛湿养肝

Prefer:

- "医疗需求"
- "健康相关消费"
- "医疗器械/药品需要资质和监管"
- "不做医疗建议"

### Education / Exams

Avoid:

- 包过、保过、必上岸、内部真题、泄题
- 花钱拿证、包就业、包分配、百分百录取、花钱提分

Prefer:

- "学习方法"
- "经验分享"
- "长期积累"

### False Scarcity / Aggressive Marketing

Avoid:

- 亏本清仓、厂家倒闭、跳楼价、疯抢
- 万人空巷、最后一波、错过再无
- 不买亏一年、仅此一天、库存告急、爆单、限时秒杀

Prefer:

- "这期先讲框架"
- "后续逐个拆解"
- "欢迎评论区投票"

### Superstition / Fortune Telling

Avoid:

- 算命、八字、看相、转运、改运、辟邪
- 化煞、镇宅、旺夫、化解小人、招财
- 破太岁、开光、符咒、挡灾消灾

Prefer:

- "心理学"
- "行为金融"
- "人性需求"
- "风险偏好"

### Abuse / Discrimination / Low-Taste Content

Avoid:

- 人身攻击、地域歧视、性别歧视、身材羞辱
- 恶意嘲讽、低俗暗示、擦边黑话

Prefer:

- "这种心理很常见"
- "我自己也会有这种情绪"
- "我们一起观察人性"

## Topic Guardrails

Avoid or handle with extreme care:

- Political red lines, maps, sovereignty, leaders, public policy mockery.
- Disaster rumors, unverified social incidents, public-institution accusations.
- Guns, controlled knives, drugs, gambling, drunk driving, dangerous challenges.
- Privacy exposure: ID numbers, phone numbers, addresses, private chat logs.
- Sexualized content, suggestive costumes, adult-service hints.
- Minors smoking, drinking, paying large tips, dangerous acts, consumer inducement.
- Copyright infringement: unauthorized film/music/creator clips.
- AI-generated content without disclosure when the platform requires labeling.
- Brand attacks, fabricated reviews, competitor smearing, "踩一捧一".
- Social conflict farming: gender, region, doctor-patient, teacher-student antagonism.
- Counterfeit, high-imitation, smuggling, fake currency.
- Medical beauty, drugs, surgery, folk remedies without qualifications.
- Fearmongering about illness, disasters, economy, or markets.

## Publishing Checklist

Before publishing:

1. Remove absolute claims: "最", "第一", "唯一", "百分百", "零风险".
2. Remove return promises: "稳赚", "保本", "必赚", "只涨不跌".
3. Remove off-platform contact guidance: "微信", "VX", "私聊", "进群", "二维码".
4. Add compliance line for finance content: "不荐股，不预测，不构成投资建议。"
5. If AI-generated visuals/audio are used, add platform-required AI label or disclosure.
6. For alcohol/tobacco cases, keep the framing analytical and add risk boundaries.
7. Avoid conflict-bait, panic-bait, and unverified data.
8. Replace exact brand/contact words with category wording when the exact name is not essential.
