# Douyin Finance Growth

Use this skill for value-investing self-media on Douyin when the goal is not just make content, but make content that keeps improving from real performance feedback.

## Core principles

- Start from ordinary people's problems, not abstract philosophy.
- Prefer concrete objects: index funds, housing, cash flow, brand premiums, consumption, etc.
- Use contrast whenever possible: asset vs liability, function vs emotion, rent vs buy, index vs stock-picking.
- Make the audience feel this is about me.
- Treat each post as one controlled experiment.

## Topic selection

Choose topics that satisfy at least two of these:

- The audience can immediately relate to it.
- The title contains a clear contradiction or tension.
- The topic can be explained with one concrete example.
- The conclusion leads to an actionable decision.

Best-performing buckets for this account:

- Ordinary people's asset cognition
- Contrasting business or consumption logic
- Company and asset case studies
- Actionable investing steps for non-professionals

## Title strategy

Prefer titles that combine:

- a common object or asset
- a clear tension or misunderstanding
- a question or outcome

Examples:

- 房子一定是资产吗？
- 普通人为什么先理解指数投资？
- 为什么盲盒比U盘贵？
- 茅台、苹果、泡泡玛特为什么能溢价？

Avoid titles that are only philosophical or too abstract unless they are tied to a real-world case.

## Format strategy

- If trust matters, prioritize real voice, voiceover, or avatar口播 over pure AI voice.
- If the message is comparative or highly visual, use image carousels.
- If the content is explanatory and structured, use short narration plus cards.
- Do not default to AI voice plus popular music unless the idea is extremely visual and simple.

## Content structure

Use this base sequence:

1. Hook with contrast or a direct question
2. Explain why this matters to ordinary people
3. Use one concrete asset/company/life example
4. Give the value-investing framework
5. End with a practical next step

## Performance loop

After every post, record:

- title
- topic bucket
- format
- hook style
- play count
- like count
- save count
- comment count
- whether the creator appeared as a real person or not
- one sentence on what likely helped or hurt performance

Then choose only one variable to test next:

- title
- hook
- format
- topic bucket
- length
- presence of voice/face

## Iteration rules

- Keep what works; change one thing at a time.
- When a topic wins, look for the underlying pattern, not just the surface wording.
- When a post fails, decide whether the issue was topic, title, format, or trust signal.
- Use the previous best-performing post as the closest reference point for the next test.

## Guardrails

- Do not present personalized investment advice as certainty.
- Keep the tone educational, not manipulative.
- Use your own framework consistently so the account feels like one person with a clear viewpoint.
